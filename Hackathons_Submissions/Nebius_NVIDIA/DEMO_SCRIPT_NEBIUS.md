## Spoken script (approx. 380 words)

After the client leaves, the demand letter, the diary date, and the thing that must stay private are still in someone’s head. HakiScribe is the legal work agent in the room.

We sign in and open the live Nemotron demo — a short Kilimani construction meeting in English and Kiswahili. Speakers are already named. This line about the partner’s tax position is privileged: you can still see it, struck through. It will not go to the model.

We run Detect. NVIDIA Nemotron Super on Nebius Token Factory proposes an Action Tray — not a chat — each card grounded in a spoken line. Here is the demand letter card; View source jumps to the quote.

We generate on Nemotron Ultra. The draft is editable. The footer shows the model id on Token Factory. Nothing is auto-served.

Research uses Tavily over Kenyan legal domains, then Super synthesises. Sources are labelled external authorities — not evidence, not verification.

The lawyer chooses what leaves the desk. That is HakiScribe.
